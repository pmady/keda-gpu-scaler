Point a KEDA ScaledObject at it with an external trigger:

  triggers:
    - type: external
      metadata:
        scalerAddress: "{{ include "keda-gpu-scaler.fullname" . }}.{{ .Release.Namespace }}.svc.cluster.local:{{ .Values.grpc.port }}"
        profile: "vllm-queue-depth"
        vllmEndpoint: "http://<vllm-service>.<namespace>.svc:8000"

Profiles: vllm-queue-depth, vllm-inference, ollama, tgi-inference,
          triton-queue-wait, triton-request-rate, triton-inference,
          training, distributed-training, batch
The vllm-* and triton-* queue profiles need vllmEndpoint or tritonEndpoint.
Or set metricType, targetValue and activationThreshold directly; see
docs/configuration.md.

Check the pods. READY 1/1 means the gRPC health check passed, which in turn
means NVML (or the synthetic collector) answered:

  kubectl get pods -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} -l {{ include "keda-gpu-scaler.selectorLabels" . | replace ": " "=" | replace "\n" "," }} -o wide

If a pod is not ready, its log says why:

  kubectl logs -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} -l app.kubernetes.io/name={{ include "keda-gpu-scaler.name" . }}
